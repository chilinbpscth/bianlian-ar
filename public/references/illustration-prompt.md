# 關羽啟發・三步教學插圖

新繪教學簡化圖，不是傳統面譜原樣複製。

Use case: scientific-educational. Create one clean portrait classroom illustration, a vertical three-step drawing guide for Hong Kong primary students, teaching a SIMPLIFIED Guan Yu-inspired Sichuan opera face pattern. White background, three identical upright oval mask shapes vertically stacked with ample white spacing; flat red and black only, very simple bold hand-drawable lines, no photorealism, no person, no ears, no costume, no headdress, no beard extending outside mask. All three masks have identical small white almond eye openings and small white oval mouth opening. Top mask: just solid red oval with fine dark outline and white eye/mouth openings. Middle mask: precisely same oval plus two thick curved black 'silkworm' eyebrows above elongated eyes and two simple black lines framing eyes, small single black nose stroke. Bottom mask: identical to middle plus THREE simple upright black strokes between the eyebrows rising toward forehead. These are cumulative steps, not different character masks. Each mask large and central, occupies about 75% width within its step. Put only a small numeral 1, 2, 3 respectively to left of each step. No Chinese text, no explanatory paragraphs, no watermark, no arrows. Warm approachable textbook vector-like bitmap, minimum detail children can copy. This is a newly authored pedagogical simplification, not a historical facsimile.
